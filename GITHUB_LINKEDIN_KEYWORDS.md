## LinkedIn company About text (English)

Hope Intelligence develops data-driven approaches to energy intelligence, Python automation, data engineering and applied machine learning. Our open-source Energy Intelligence research project explores Germany's electricity market using public Bundesnetzagentur SMARD datasets: wholesale power prices, renewable generation, negative-price events, historical volatility and transparent forecasting. We turn complex data into usable dashboards and reproducible engineering workflows. We welcome collaboration and feedback from utilities, energy suppliers, renewable-energy developers, industrial energy managers and technology teams.
